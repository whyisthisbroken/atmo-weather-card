Your station sets the numbers, you tune the rest.

Connect your own sensors and the card shows what they measure. It goes further than the numbers: your cloud cover sets the clouds, your rain gauge how hard it rains, your visibility the haze and your wind where it all drifts. Whether it rains or snows still comes from the weather entity. Then tune the weather and let it settle: every control in the demo is a real option from the editor, and the YAML tab shows the card you built.

Set it up without writing YAML.

  • Chips for any entity, attribute or forecast, as text, ring or bar.
  • Layouts: Standard, Compact, Wide, Home with a house of its own and Panel, each a finished look in one click.

  • English or German, following your Home Assistant language.

Before you start, check two things.

Required

Most installs have both already.

  • A weather.* entityFrom any weather integration. When it reports cloud cover, wind, visibility, humidity or dew point, the sky uses them.
  • Home Assistant 2026.11 or newerThe card installs from the Marketplace. sun.sun, on by default, moves the sun and brings the golden and the blue hour.

Nice to have

Each one adds to the sky, none is needed.

  • Moon phaseThe Moon integration gives the moon its real phase.
  • A rain sensor in mm/hRain, snow and sleet then follow the measured amount.
  • Your weather stationTemperature, humidity, wind, cloud cover or visibility from your own sensors.

Running in three steps, from the Marketplace.

Find the card

In Home Assistant open Settings › Marketplace and search for Atmo Weather Card, or open it there directly:

Install the card

Open it, select Install, then reload your dashboard.

Add it to a dashboard

Add a card, search for Atmo Weather Card, or paste this:

type: custom:atmo-weather-card
weather_entity: weather.home

No Marketplace? For the manual install you copy the files of a release into www and add one resource entry.

Good to know.

Does it work with my weather integration?

Yes, with any weather.* entity. Cloud cover, wind, visibility and dew point are used when your integration provides them, and skipped when it does not.

Will it slow down my wall tablet?

Pick the Low preset for older tablets. The card also pauses when it is off screen or the tab is hidden.

Does it work with imperial units?

Yes. Wind, temperature and rain keep the units of your entities, mph and °F included.

Can I use my own picture of my house?

Yes, a day and a night image sit in front of the sky, pictures can change with the weather or any entity, and the Home layout places the house for you; until you add yours, it shows a house of its own. See Custom house image.

Does the card send data anywhere?

No. It loads nothing from other servers and talks only to your Home Assistant.

Which languages does the editor speak?

English and German, following your Home Assistant language. Everything else shows English.
